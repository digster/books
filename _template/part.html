Part templates, one per kind

What these are

Three part templates, one for each kind of material, plus the book index template and meta.json. They double as the stylesheet's proving ground: between them they exercise every component in assets/style.css. Copy the variant that matches the part you are writing and replace the placeholder prose — which is written about the template precisely so that no book content lives here.

The shared skeleton

Every part page, whatever its kind, has the same bones: the stylesheet and theme.js in the head, a skip link, a breadcrumb header ending in the theme switch, one <h1>, an article.part carrying data-kind, the six sections in order, a pager, and a site footer. Only the contents of #read differ by kind, and the difference is not cosmetic — it is what the kind is for.

Most parts are mixed. Declare the dominant kind in data-kind and satisfy the requirements of any kind genuinely present — a mechanism part that also lays out a small landscape needs axes as well as a ratchet.

Copying a template

  1. Copy the variant matching the part's dominant kind into books/<slug>/NN-<part-slug>.html.
  2. Fix the two relative paths: the stylesheet becomes ../../assets/style.css and the breadcrumb gains a level. Templates sit one directory shallower than parts do.
  3. Replace the .eyebrow cells, the <h1>, and the <title>.
  4. Build the decomposition and the concrete run first. They are the spine; prose written before them tends to describe conclusions rather than reach them.
  5. Point the pager at the real neighbours, and update the previous part's rel="next" to match.
  6. Run uv run tools/check.py.

Note

The brief lists a single part.html holding all three variants. Three part titles in one file would mean three <h1>s, which the same brief forbids — or demoting every section heading a level, which would stop the real CSS selectors from being exercised at all. Splitting into three files keeps both rules intact and makes each variant a working page rather than a fragment.

A note on the placeholder prose

The filler in these templates describes what belongs in the slot it occupies, rather than being lorem ipsum. That keeps two promises at once: no book content appears in the bootstrap, and the typography is tested against real English — with the em-dashes, numerals, italicised terms, inline code, and footnote markers that actually stress a stylesheet.

What these templates do not decide

Part boundaries, ordering, and kind are decisions made in the coverage plan, before any template is copied. A template can enforce that a claim part has a contrast case; it cannot tell you whether this material is a claim.

Notes

These pages are deliberately excluded from the shelf. They are development scaffolding, and _template/ is not linked from index.html — reach them from the README or directly.
