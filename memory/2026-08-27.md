## *Surfing Uncertainty* (Task B)

Andy Clark, OUP 2016, xviii + 401pp, ISBN 978-0-19-021701-3. Ten parts,
**34,569 words**, ~198 min. `books/surfing-uncertainty/`.

**Coverage:** 11 full, 1 condensed (ch 10 — its evaluative half belongs in the
afterword), 2 folded (both appendices, into Parts 2 and 3), 0 read-it. No
reordering: Clark's sequence *is* the escalation.

**Kinds:** 4 mechanism (2 the machine, 3 precision, 4 the imaginarium,
5 active inference), 4 claim (1 the thesis, 6 social flow, 7 representation,
9 the dark room), 2 terrain (8 the clinical landscape, 10 cultural scaffolds).

### Decisions worth keeping

- **Part structure settled at a checkpoint before writing**, per §13 step 2,
  because slugs are permanent. One part per chapter, preface+intro as Part 1.
- **The TOC had to be established from a primary source.** Search snippets
  contradicted each other and invented chapter titles ("Adjusting the Helm",
  an introduction called "The Ambitious Thesis"). Matteo Colombo's review in
  *Minds & Machines* (2017), extracted from PDF with `pypdf`, settled the part
  structure (I = chs 1–3, II = 4–7, III = 8–10) against a reviewer who had it
  wrong, and supplied chapter-level content plus the page-cited quotes.
- **Every citation verified via the PubMed E-utilities API**, not from recall.
  This caught two errors: Merckelbach & van de Ven is in *J Behav Ther Exp
  Psychiatry*, not *Behaviour Research and Therapy*; and two PMIDs I guessed
  returned a paper on nitrite reductase and one on the Emperor Seamounts.
  **Never guess an identifier.**
- **All four traces executed before writing and re-executed against the
  published pages afterwards.** The Part 3 cue-combination weights flip
  exactly 0.80/0.20 → 0.20/0.80 when visual σ goes 1.5 → 6.0 mm, which is the
  Ernst & Banks result falling out of the arithmetic.
